How it works

Create Box puts a shape layer behind the selected layers and parents them to it. The layers get no expressions: animate them as usual. Moving, scaling or rotating the box moves the group. Any layer parented to a box is in that box, so editing text, renaming or reordering layers is safe. Use Add to Box and Remove to change what's in a box.

Smart Animate follows the layers' keyframes, not every frame. A move that brings a layer into view (from clear of where it ends up, or fading or scaling in) brings it in; a move that takes it clear away (or fades or scales it out, or erases it) takes it out, and so does its out point; smaller moves reposition it. The box morphs to fit the layers that are in, each on its own curve, growing from the side a layer comes from (Grow From), and keeps a leaving layer's space until the next one comes in (or shrinks away with it: When Empty). Layers without keyframes, and text changes, get Morph Time.

In Box adds a menu to the selected layers: leave it on Auto, or keyframe In / Out to say yourself when a layer counts. Freeze, in the box's Effect Controls, holds the box. Matte uses the box as its layers' track matte (After Effects 2023+); click it again to take the mattes off.

The look lives in the box's Contents: Fill, Stroke, Gradient Fill, Gradient Stroke and the rectangle's Roundness, native and yours to edit or animate. Gradients stretch with the box: their Start and End Point are percentages of it. Padding, size and animation settings are in the box's Effect Controls.

Drag a blue value left or right to change it (Shift for bigger steps, Ctrl/Cmd for finer ones), or click it to type. These panel values are the defaults for new boxes; Apply to Box pushes them onto selected boxes (animated properties are left alone).

Release bakes the box's size into plain values and keyframes and removes the rig.
